On the phone, Veto reads every new SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram notification by itself, checks unknown callers before they ring and pops Kiki up on top of the app you are in. No copying, no pasting. You switch on each guard once and the permissions do the rest. A browser is not allowed to read notifications or calls, so here you paste or type a message yourself; every check below runs the same engine, on this device.

Why it is not on Google Play yet: Google grants SMS, notification and call powers only after it reviews an app on Google Play, and phones block or limit them for apps installed from elsewhere. Veto is a hackathon build that has not been through that review yet. You can still try the Android app (veto.apk) with the 3 install steps.
