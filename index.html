Scams do not wait for you to ask

Most checkers make you copy a suspicious message into an app and ask "is this fake?". By then people have usually tapped. Veto sits on top of your messages and calls, checks each one as it arrives, and only appears when something needs you.

Works fully offline
Every check runs on the phone: about 1 millisecond per link, no internet, no account, no server. Nothing you receive is uploaded.

📩 Messages

SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram notifications are checked the moment they arrive. A red banner says why it is a scam, with Report, Warn family and the police number.

🔗 Links

Every link is judged: fake bank and government sites, look-alike names, short links, cheap domain endings. 98.3 % precision on 33,766 test links.

📞 Calls

Unknown callers are checked before you answer. If a caller pushes you to open UAE PASS, your bank app or a remote-control app, Veto steps in.

🛡 The UAE rulebook

Veto knows what 77 UAE banks, police and government bodies never ask for, and their real numbers and websites. A message that breaks those rules is caught.

Install in one minute

  1. Download the app

    Tap Download for Android on your phone.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks to allow installs from your browser: tap Settings, switch on "Allow from this source", go back and tap Install. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway (Veto is a hackathon build, not yet on the Play Store).

  3. Open Veto and switch on the guards

    The Guarding screen lists each permission and why it is needed. Turn each one on. Veto then hides and only pops up when something needs you.

  4. Done

    Send yourself a test: a text like "Your parcel is on hold, pay 12 AED at http://emiratespost-redelivery.top" and watch the warning appear.
